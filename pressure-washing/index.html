How to price a pressure washing job

Count equipment wear as a cost

Pumps, hoses, surface cleaners and trailers wear out. Divide what you expect to spend on repairs and replacement in a year by the jobs you do, and put that in overhead for every job.

Price by time on site

Square-foot rates are a shortcut, but your costs come from hours. Estimate setup, washing, rinsing and pack-up time, and let the calculator turn hours into a price that keeps your margin.

Set a minimum job price

Short jobs still cost travel and setup. If the calculator comes out under your minimum, quote the minimum.
