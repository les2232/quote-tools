How to price a lawn care job

Drive time is part of the job

Travel between yards is paid time with no mowing. Put it in travel cost, or routes full of small yards will quietly lose money.

Quote per visit, bill per month if you like

Price one visit from its costs, then multiply by visits in the month for a flat monthly bill. Customers like one number; you keep the per-visit margin.

Re-quote overgrown yards

A first cut after weeks of growth can take twice as long. Quote it as its own job with more hours.
